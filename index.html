Three evaluations

Cue recognition

An edited-footage diagnostic tests which way a short sequence runs and where it occurs.

Reply prediction

A video-conditioned adapter predicts recorded reply tokens across 2,031 held-out video turns.

Perceived empathy

Recipients rate a live Gemma conversation. The experimental arm adds pacing cues, preference cards and repetition screening.
